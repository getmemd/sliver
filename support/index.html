Blocking

How does blocking work?

You choose apps (and optionally categories or websites) in the Screen Time system picker. They stay blocked by Sliver's block screen until you complete your daily goal. Review the required number of photos, and your chosen apps open for the rest of the day. A new day starts at midnight, and the apps are blocked again until you reach the next goal.

You can change the goal size in Settings → Daily goal, from 10 to 100 photos (30 by default). If fewer photos are left to review than your goal, the goal shrinks to match: review the rest and it counts. Once everything is reviewed, Sliver offers to let you go through your photos again.

The block screen has two buttons: “Start Review” and “I Need It Now”. They open Sliver through a notification, so notifications need to be allowed for them to work.

Why can't I lower my goal or remove an app from the list?

This protects you from yourself. You can loosen blocking (lower the goal, remove apps from the list) only after you've completed today's goal. You can tighten it at any time.

What is emergency access?

If you really need an app right now, tap “I Need It Now” on the block screen or open Emergency access in Sliver's settings. After a 30-second wait, your chosen apps open for 5 minutes.

You can use emergency access 3 times a day. It doesn't count toward your daily goal. Once you've used it up, “I Need It Now” disappears from the block screen until the next day.

Sliver says “Blocking is off”. How do I restore Screen Time access?

This means Screen Time access was revoked, so apps aren't being blocked. On the Today screen, tap “Turn On Again” and allow access in the system dialog.

If the system dialog doesn't appear, make sure Screen Time is turned on in iOS Settings, then try again.

Sliver says the app list is corrupted

Sliver couldn't read your saved app selection. Tap “Select” and choose your apps again. If you haven't completed today's goal yet, you can do this after you complete it, because choosing again before then counts as loosening the block.

Photos and storage

I deleted photos, but storage wasn't freed. Why?

Deleted photos aren't erased right away. iOS moves them to the Recently Deleted album so you can recover them. They stay there for up to 30 days, and then iOS deletes them permanently. Only then is the space freed on your device and in iCloud.

To free up space right away:

  1. Open the Photos app.
  2. Find the Recently Deleted album (under Utilities). iOS may ask for Face ID or your passcode.
  3. Tap Select → Delete All and confirm.

Keep in mind that you can't recover photos after this. The amount of space Sliver shows is an estimate.

I swiped photos to the trash, but they're still in my library

Photos are deleted with a single system confirmation at the end of a review session. If you cancel that confirmation or choose “Later”, the photos stay in a queue. A banner showing how many photos are waiting to be deleted appears on the Today screen, with a “Delete Now” button.

How do I give Sliver full photo access?

Reviewing and deleting photos requires full access. If you allowed access only to selected photos, or denied access, Sliver shows an “Open Settings” button. It opens Sliver's page in iOS Settings, where you choose Photos → Full Access.

Why are some photos missing from the review?

Favorite and hidden photos aren't included. Neither are photos that can't be deleted from here, such as those in shared albums. When you've reviewed everything, Sliver shows “All reviewed”, and you can go through your photos again. Photos you kept go back into the queue, and your stats and streak stay.

Sliver Pro and purchases

What is Sliver Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no subscription. Blocking, photo review, and deletion are free without Pro.

Can I share Pro with my family?

Yes, Pro is available through Family Sharing. If the family organizer has turned on purchase sharing, family members get Pro without buying it separately.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy Pro, then tap “Restore Purchases” in Sliver's settings. You won't be charged again.

Your data

What data does Sliver collect?

None. The app runs on your device and sends nothing to us. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. All of Sliver's data is stored only on your device, and iOS erases it along with the app. The app's blocking also stops when you delete it. Photos you kept are left unchanged. Photos you already deleted stay in Recently Deleted under the Photos app's rules.
